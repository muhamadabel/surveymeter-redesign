About us

From the IFLS field team to a policy research institute

SurveyMETER was founded to formalise the work of a research team with more than 15 years of experience collecting and analysing Indonesia Family Life Survey (IFLS) data. Our aim is accurate, reliable data for sustainable public policy.

  1. 2002

    Founded as a foundation in Yogyakarta on 20 February 2002.

  2. 2009

    Foundation re-established by a new notarial deed, registered with the Ministry of Law and Human Rights.

  3. 2011

    Became a research institute: research, community assistance, and policy advocacy.

  4. 2020

    Became PT SurveyMETER to widen work with government, the private sector, and international partners.

Vision

A knowledge institution that inspires and strengthens policy based on research data.

Mission

  1. Run large-scale surveys and research objectively and with quality.
  2. Run innovative independent studies.
  3. Take an active part in policy dialogue.
  4. Provide technical assistance on using data and research results to policymakers and other knowledge institutions.

Values

  • Scientific integrity: objective, high quality, independent
  • Humanity
  • Neutral and impartial

Our work

Three areas of work

The Indonesian government and international agencies have repeatedly trusted us with surveys, evaluations, and training.

01

Surveys

Impact evaluation studies
Measuring the impact of government or international programmes, both ongoing and completed.
Policy-oriented studies
Assessing how well policies work, as a basis for improving programmes.
02

Training and development

Training
Training interviewers, field staff, data editors, and cadres for partner surveys.
Regional assistance
A social laboratory in assisted areas to test empowerment tools and support data-based local policy.
03

Research

Scientific research
Longitudinal household surveys with Indonesian and international researchers, some with public data.
Studies and knowledge management
Independent studies, policy briefs, and popular writing from research results.

Public data

Panel data for research and policy

Indonesia Longitudinal Aging Survey 2023

Tracks the condition of older Indonesians to support social security and health reform for older people, and measures indicators of the National Strategy on Ageing (Perpres 88/2021).

Context
Indonesians aged 60+ numbered 30.1 million (10.8%) in 2022, projected at 21.9% by 2050
Partners
Bappenas, Asian Development Bank
Download the report ILAS data page

Eastern Indonesia Household Life Survey

A survey of households, individuals, communities, and health and education facilities, based on the IFLS design. Fieldwork in 2012.

Sample
About 10,000 individuals, 2,500 households, 99 communities
Provinces
East Nusa Tenggara, East Kalimantan, Southeast Sulawesi, Maluku, North Maluku, West Papua, Papua
Partners
TNP2K, PRSF, AusAID
Open the data page

The Study of the Tsunami Aftermath and Recovery (STAR)

A long-term panel on economic recovery, housing relocation, and social resilience in Aceh after the tsunami.

Partners
Duke University, World Bank
Related work
Measuring Disaster Recovery (2023), tsunami exposure and the HPA axis 14 years later (2023)
