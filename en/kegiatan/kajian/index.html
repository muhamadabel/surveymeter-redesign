Research

Study

The Division Study of SurveyMETER is instigating scientific activities in capacity building both internally and externally. Internal capacity building is carried out through writing papers, journals, result studies, research reports, policy briefs, seminars, workshops, etc., implemented within the internal scope of SurveyMETER itself. It is the motivation for SurveyMETER to vigorously conduct self-initiated research for knowledge products as well as commissioned research by clients. For the external ones, it is carried out by sending works of writing (papers, journals, policy briefs) to scientific publications both nationally and internationally and attending seminars and workshops both nationally and globally. It expects that SurveyMETER in addition to having publications of knowledge and documenting the results of the research conducted, at the same time advances to maintain and improve the skills of staff in conducting scientific studies.
