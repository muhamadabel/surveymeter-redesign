Training of Enumerators SUSERDES-KOMPAK 2015

  • Tanggal : 30/11/2015 - 10/12/2015
  • Lokasi : Grand Wahid Hotel Kota Salatiga, Jawa Tengah

The 2015 Rural Household Socio-Economic Survey (SUSERDES) entered the Training of Enumerators (ToE) stage. It was held at the Grand Wahid Hotel, Salatiga City, from November 30 to December 10, 2015, and attended by 63 prospective field assistants.

Prior to conducting this ToE, SurveyMETER held the Training of Trainers (ToT) from November 23 - 28, 2015, at our office in Yogyakarta, and attended by 14 prospective field supervisors.

The implementation of SUSERDES 2015 is a collaboration among SurveyMETER, the Ministry of National Development Planning/BAPPENAS, and the KOMPAK Program.

(Governance for Growth - Community Collaboration and Services for Welfare). KOMPAK is a collaborative partnership program between the Government of Australia and the Government of Indonesia that started in January 2015. It aims to improve access and quality of basic services and increase economic opportunities for the people of Indonesia in the context of implementing the 2015-2019 National Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMN). (JF)
